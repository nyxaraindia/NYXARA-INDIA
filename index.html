A small studio building across the whole stack.

NYXARA INDIA is a technology company founded by Amal K Aji. We make software that thinks: apps with AI built in, machine learning products, story-driven games and systems that are secure from the first line of code.

One team covers research, design and engineering, so an idea can move from a sketch to a working product on every platform without being handed between vendors.

CurVitae, our AI-powered CV builder, is our first released product. More are on the way, and you can see them under Projects.
